# Plan 1.1: Build TileEstimatorCalculator Component

## Objective
Build an interactive, mathematically accurate Tile Coverage & Box Estimator component (`src/components/TileEstimatorCalculator.tsx`) that enables homeowners, architects, and contractors to calculate room square footage, tile piece counts, packing boxes, and recommended cutting wastage margins.

## Context
- `.gsd/SPEC.md`
- `.gsd/ARCHITECTURE.md`
- `src/data/showroomData.ts`
- `src/index.css`

## Tasks

  <name>Create TileEstimatorCalculator component</name>
  <files>
    src/components/TileEstimatorCalculator.tsx
  </files>
  <action>
    Build `TileEstimatorCalculator.tsx` with:
    1. Unit switch: Feet (ft) vs Meters (m).
    2. Dimension inputs (length, width) with quick room presets (Living Room, Master Bed, Bathroom, Kitchen).
    3. Tile format selector matching real verified dimensions (600x1200mm, 600x600mm, 300x600mm, 200x1200mm, 400x400mm, 75x300mm).
    4. Wastage margin selector (5%, 10% recommended, 15% herringbone/diagonal).
    5. Calculation output cards displaying net area, gross area, total pieces, and boxes needed.
    6. Direct WhatsApp dispatch trigger with pre-filled quantities.
    7. Consultation modal trigger to pass calculated quantities into the enquiry form.
    
    AVOID: Arbitrary unverified packaging math.
    USE: Standard Indian tile packaging coverage formulas (e.g., 600x1200mm = 2 pcs/box = 1.44 m² ≈ 15.5 sq ft).
